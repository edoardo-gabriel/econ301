About this site

A free, unofficial study companion for ECON 301. It turns the ideas from lecture and the handouts into graphs you can move, questions you can answer, and exercises with full solutions.

How to use it

  • Topics (top bar): one page per topic, in the order we cover them. Read the short explanation, then play with the interactive graphs until the idea clicks.
  • Pop quizzes appear as you scroll, and the Quiz me button gives you a question on the page you are reading.
  • Worked exercises on each page have a Show solution button. Try the problem on paper first.
  • Exercises (top bar): every question in one place, a 10-question mock quiz, and numeric drills that generate new numbers every time.
  • Dark mode: use the ◐ button at the right of the top bar.

Your progress and privacy

There are no accounts and nothing is sent anywhere. Which questions you have answered correctly is saved only in your own browser on this device, so your instructors cannot see it, and clearing your browser data (or the reset button on the Exercises page) erases it.

A note on accuracy

This site is a study aid, not course material. If anything here disagrees with lecture or the handouts, lecture and the handouts are right. If you spot a mistake, please tell your TA.

Who made it

Built for ECON 301 (Fall 2026, instructor Fran Flanagan) and maintained by your TA, Edoardo Gabriel. Math is typeset with KaTeX; everything else is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
